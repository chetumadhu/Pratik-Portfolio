/PROJECT BRIEF

Brand Essence & Philosophical Foundation

Decorbvibes is a premium home decor brand curating culturally inspired artifacts that blend craftsmanship, storytelling, and modern aesthetics. The brand focuses on transforming living spaces into expressive environments through timeless decor pieces that feel elegant, artistic, and emotionally connected.

Brand Purpose

At DecorVibes, our purpose is to bring culture, craftsmanship, and contemporary elegance into everyday spaces. We create decor that tells a story, bridges timeless heritage with modern sensibilities, and transforms living environments into authentic reflections of style and comfort.

Brand Vision

To redefine modern living through culturally inspired luxury décor that transforms spaces into meaningful experiences. DecorVibes aspires to be the premier destination where timeless artisanal craftsmanship meets contemporary elegance.

Brand Mission

To curate and deliver culturally inspired luxury décor that seamlessly blends timeless craftsmanship with modern aesthetics, creating elevated living experiences for contemporary homes.

/KEY HIGHLIGHTS

Visual Identity Architecture & Touchpoints

The Logo Mark: Fusion of Letter 'D' & Silhouette of an Artifact

The DecorVibes logo mark is a fusion of the letter ‘D’ and the silhouette of a decorative artifact. The sculptural form symbolizes cultural richness and timeless elegance, while the grounded base reflects warmth, stability, and authenticity.

Logo Mark Construction: Geometric Harmony of Letter 'D' & Artifact Form

Pattern Architecture: The DecorVibes pattern is derived from the logo's geometric elements — the 'D' form, circles, and semicircles creating an evocative, luxurious visual texture.
