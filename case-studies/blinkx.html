/PROJECT BRIEF

Project Overview & Core Mission

BlinkX aimed to democratise equity investments for new-age users by providing a beginner-friendly platform that blends intuitive design with powerful financial tools. We partnered with BlinkX to build a user-centric investment app that simplifies the complexities of stock trading, especially for Gen-Z and millennial audiences.

The Challenges

⚠ Unintuitive Navigation

Users had difficulty finding essential actions like portfolio view, order history, or live market updates due to cluttered, complex layouts.

⚠ Inconsistent Visual Identity

Early designs lacked brand personality and failed to emotionally connect with younger, digitally savvy retail investors.

⚠ Low Educational Engagement

Users were skipping or ignoring long, intimidating blocks of text explaining equity fundamentals and risk disclosures.

Objective & Goal

The goal was to revamp the platform to make financial discovery easy, trustworthy, and personalised. We aimed to improve user retention, streamline trading flows, and create an inviting educational layer.

Solutions

  • Designed a peppy learning flow with step-by-step cards, mascot illustrations, and friendlier bottom navigation — shortening order placement time.
  • Introduced a fresh design system with gradients, rounded shapes, and cheerful avatars, creating a welcoming tone without sacrificing trading rigor.
  • Engineered a dual-experience workflow that serves beginners with plain-English insights while giving active traders advanced charting tools.
  • The result — a product that feels both playful and intuitive, winning user trust from the very first tap.
