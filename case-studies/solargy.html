THE CHALLENGE

Uncovering the Problem

Homeowners in suburban and Tier-2 Indian cities were interested in green solar power but were discouraged by technical jargon like kilowatt peaks (kWp), inverter phase matching, and confusing government subsidies (PM Surya Ghar). Most dropped off before requesting a site quotation.

Key Pain Points Identified:
  • ! High drop-off during data intake and report comprehension
  • ! Cluttered numerical interfaces leading to decision paralysis
  • ! Lack of actionable, personalized next steps for users
THE DESIGN SOLUTION

How I Approached It

I led the end-to-end design sprint from discovery interviews to high-fidelity prototyping, emphasizing cognitive ease, visual hierarchy, and instant value delivery.

  • ✓ Created an interactive 3-step visual calculator where users input only their monthly electricity bill amount to instantly view estimated savings and payback months.
  • ✓ Integrated satellite rooftop visualizer allowing homeowners to preview panel placement directly on their roof structure.
  • ✓ Structured a transparent subsidy and zero-cost EMI financing breakdown that compares electricity bills before vs after solar installation.
  • ✓ Simplified technician site survey scheduling into a single WhatsApp-verified one-tap booking flow.

“Clean energy technology shouldn’t require an engineering degree to understand. Solargy made clean energy adoption as simple and joyful as ordering online.”
